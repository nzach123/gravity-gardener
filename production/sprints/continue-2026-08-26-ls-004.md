### The eight acceptance criteria, in brief

1. `LevelRoot._ready()` constructs `LevelState(buckets_total)` and
   `OxygenState(capacity, tuning)` from its own `@export`s at step (a), before
   any binding
2. `buckets_total` comes from `LevelValidation.count_buckets()` — the same
   primitive `validate()` uses — **not** a group count
3. `Player`/`PlayerWateringComponent`, `Goal`, `HUD`, `OxygenDrain` each receive
   state through a `bind(...)` call from `LevelRoot._ready()`
4. Every bound consumer refuses to operate before `bind()` ran, with
   `push_error()` **and** an early `return`
5. Each `Plant.pour_completed` is connected by `LevelRoot` to
   `LevelState.consume_bucket()`; `Plant` receives no state object at all
6. `OxygenDrain` is a child of `LevelRoot`, **not** of `Player`
7. Every bound consumer is a descendant of `LevelRoot` (the A2-03 invariant)
8. The group-based plant discovery in `main.gd` is replaced by a recursive type
   scan

### Seams to leave, not fill

- **Step (b) — `LevelValidation.validate()`.** Leave a named, commented
  insertion point citing **LV-005** and ADR-0003 D3.1. Do **not** call
  `validate()` here. Record the ordering LV-005 will assert: `validate()` runs
  BEFORE the state objects are constructed, reading only raw authored `@export`
  scene data.
- **Steps (e) and (f)** belong to `gravity-authority`. `main.gd` already does a
  version of both. Do not rewrite them.
- Comment at the `HUD` bind site that a persistent or cross-scene HUD would hold
  a stale `LevelState` with **no error and no crash** — `RefCounted` leaks are
  invisible and there is no watchdog. The Presentation epic is the one most
  likely to break this.

### Four engine facts the story says were verified on 2026-08-14, not recalled

- `_ready()` is **bottom-up**, and autoloads run before the scene. This is the
  entire reason `bind()` exists instead of consumers pulling state.
- Signal connections to a `RefCounted` are **weak** and do not keep it alive.
  That is what makes `Plant.pour_completed` → `LevelState.consume_bucket()` safe
  across a scene reload.
- `push_error()` logs but does **not** pause execution. It is a logging call,
  not an exception. Without the early `return` the consumer carries on against
  an unset reference — a null dereference, or a silent read of default field
  values instead of the refusal AC-4 promises.
- **`assert()` compiles out entirely in release exports.** Every bind guard in
  the project would silently vanish from the shipped build. Use `push_error()`.
  This is A2-02, recorded specifically so nobody swaps in `assert()` later as an
  apparent cleanup. If a reviewer suggests that swap, this is the answer.
